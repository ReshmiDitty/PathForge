Frequently Asked Questions

What is the "Demo Checkout" mode?

PathForge includes a built-in instant Demo Checkout simulator. If you do not have Razorpay test API keys set up, you can simply click "Pay in Demo Mode" at checkout to instantly simulate a real INR transaction and unlock features without needing any credit card or gateway credentials!

How do student coupons work?

Students can enter coupon codes such as STUDENT20 for an instant 20% discount, or STARTUP50 for 50% off during demo presentation!

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?

Yes! You can cancel your subscription with 1-click on your Billing Page at any time. Your account will automatically return to the Free plan.

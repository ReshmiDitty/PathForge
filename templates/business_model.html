Viva Presentation Defense

How to Explain This Business Model to Your Professor

PathForge is not merely an EdTech content website—it is an execution operating system. While competitors monetize passive video lectures, PathForge monetizes actionable student artifacts (validated canvases, radar reports, and pitch decks) that students directly submit for grading and external grant funding.

Low CAC Moat: Classroom assignments mandate Lean Canvas creation, giving PathForge zero-cost cohort distribution.
Dual-Path Moat: Eliminates founder fear by preserving a parallel corporate safety net.
High Margins: Pure software platform with no physical inventory and 82% gross margins.

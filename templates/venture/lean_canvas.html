💡 Viva & Exam Concept: Why Ash Maurya's Lean Canvas over Alex Osterwalder's Business Model Canvas?

The traditional Business Model Canvas (BMC) was created for established enterprises and includes Key Partners, Key Activities, Key Resources, and Customer Relationships. In early-stage startups, these are unknowns. Ash Maurya replaced those four blocks with Problem, Solution, Key Metrics, and Unfair Advantage, shifting the focus to problem-solution fit and rapid falsification.

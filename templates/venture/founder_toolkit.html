Founder Toolkit & Indian Support Ecosystem

Everything a student founder must know about startup financing stages, non-dilutive government schemes, top Indian incubators, and Intellectual Property Rights (IPR).

1 Startup Funding Stages: From Idea to IPO

Stage 0

Bootstrapping / FFF

Founders fund early prototypes from personal savings, college competitions, or Friends, Family & Fools. Retains 100% equity.

Typical Ticket: ₹0 - ₹5 Lakhs
Stage 1

Pre-Seed / Grants

Non-dilutive government grants (SISFS, NIDHI-PRAYAS) and micro-angels to build an MVP and conduct customer interviews.

Typical Ticket: ₹10L - ₹50 Lakhs
Stage 2

Seed & Angel Networks

Angel investors (Indian Angel Network, Mumbai Angels) back early product-market fit (PMF) and initial paying customer traction.

Typical Ticket: ₹50L - ₹2.5 Crores
Stage 3+

Venture Capital (Series A-C)

Institutional VC funds (Peak XV, Elevation, Accel) invest to rapidly scale customer acquisition and national distribution.

Typical Ticket: ₹15 Cr - ₹100+ Crores

2 Key Indian Student Founder Support Schemes

DPIIT / Startup India Up to ₹50 Lakhs

Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)

Provides financial assistance to startups for proof of concept, prototype development, product trials, market-entry and commercialization.

Grant Component: Up to ₹20 Lakhs for validation and MVP testing.
Debt/CCD Component: Up to ₹50 Lakhs for market commercialization.
Eligibility: DPIIT recognized startup incorporated not more than 2 years ago.
DST (Dept of Science & Tech) Up to ₹10 Lakhs

NIDHI-PRAYAS (Promoting Young & Aspiring Innovators)

Specifically designed to support young students and researchers with an idea to build a physical or IoT prototype within 18 months.

Funding Type: 100% Non-dilutive Grant (Zero equity taken).
Host Centers: PRAYAS Centers across IITs, NITs, and top tech institutes.
Focus: Hardware, smart sensors, clean energy, agriculture and medical devices.
DBT (Biotechnology) Up to ₹50 Lakhs

BIRAC BIG (Biotechnology Ignition Grant)

India's flagship non-dilutive grant for healthcare, medical diagnostics, biotechnology, industrial bioprocesses, and agritech.

Grant Value: ₹50 Lakhs for 18-month project execution.
Mentorship: Top incubators like C-CAMP, Venture Center Pune, and KIIT-TBI.
Incubator Network Tier-1 Centers

Premier Indian Startup Incubators

Physical spaces providing seed funding, maker labs, legal counsel, and investor demo days:

T-Hub (Hyderabad): World's largest technology incubator campus.
NSRCEL (IIM Bangalore): Best-in-class venture acceleration & B2B mentors.
SINE (IIT Bombay): Deep-tech hardware & academic patent spin-offs.

3 Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) for Student Founders

📜

Patents

Protects novel inventions and processes for 20 years. Must meet 3 strict criteria: Novelty, Inventive Step (Non-obvious), and Industrial Applicability.

Provisional vs Complete spec
™️

Trademarks

Protects brand names, logos, slogans, and distinctive visual identities across 45 NICE classifications. Prevents consumer confusion.

TM = Pending • ® = Registered
©️

Copyright

Protects original literary, musical, and software code works automatically upon creation for author's lifetime + 60 years.

Protects source code & UI assets
🔒

Trade Secrets & NDAs

Protects confidential formulas, business processes, and customer lists through Non-Disclosure Agreements without public registration.

Governed by contract law
